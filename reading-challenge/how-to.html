HOW TO PLAY

Read books, practise times tables, catch Pokémon and win prizes!

1. Pick your name

👆
At the top, tap TAIGA, LEO or YUDAI. Always check it is your name!

📖 2. Read a book

1
Read a whole book.
2
Tap I read a book!
3
Type the book title.
4
Quiz? Pick No quiz, or pick ReadnQuiz, log in, take the quiz and type your score (like 8 out of 10). Don't look at the book!
5
Tap THROW POKé BALL!. A passed book (8 out of 10 or more with a quiz) fills your 🔴 Poké Ball by 300! 🎉

🎸 3. Times Tables (Taiga & Leo)

1
Tap Times Tables, then Open Times Tables Rock Stars.
2
Log in with your school username and password and play.
3
Come back and type how many you got right. Tap LET IT RIP!. Every correct answer fills your 🔴 Poké Ball by 2! 🎉
✨
The app adds up all your correct answers for you.

🏛️ 4. Latin & 🧮 MyMaths (Yudai)

1
Practise Latin or MyMaths.
2
Tap Latin or MyMaths and type how many you got right.
3
MyMaths: every correct answer fills your 🔴 Poké Ball by 1. Latin: by 1 for every 2! 🎉
✨
These help you catch Pokémon. Your prize comes from books.

⭐ 5. Level up & evolve your Pokémon!

1
Your ⭐ partner gets EXP every time you study:
  • 🎸 Times tables: 2 EXP for every correct answer
  • 📖 A book passed: 300 EXP
  • 🧮 MyMaths: 1 EXP for every correct answer · 🏛️ Latin: 1 EXP for every 2
2
100 EXP = 1 level. When a Pokémon reaches the right level, it can evolve, just like in the games! (Charmander evolves at Lv.16, Charmeleon at Lv.36.)
3
Tap ✨ Evolve! when it is ready. Some Pokémon (like Eevee) let you choose what they become.
💎
Every time you rank up, you also get a 💎 Mega Stone. Use it on a Pokémon that can Mega Evolve or Gigantamax (like Charizard).
👀
In your 🎒 Pokémon box, every Pokémon shows its level. ⬆ means it is ready to evolve. EXP stays with the Pokémon, even after a trade.
🏅
🔴 Poké Ball: everything you study also fills your Poké Ball (the same numbers as EXP). When it reaches 1,500, you win a reward: catch a NEW Pokémon or trade!

🗺️ Explore new regions!

1
You start in Kanto. All 1,025 Pokémon are out there, region by region!
2
Catch half of the Pokémon families in a region (a Pokémon and its evolutions are one family), and the next region opens: Kanto → Johto → Hoenn → Sinnoh → Unova → Kalos → Alola → Galar → Paldea.
👀
Your 🎒 Pokémon box shows how many you have, like 🗺️ Kanto 12 / 151.
🌟
Legendary Pokémon: get 100 or more times tables right in one go, and fill your Poké Ball with it. Sometimes a legendary Pokémon appears!
✨
Shiny Pokémon have different colours and are very rare. Times tables make them more than twice as easy to find!
🎲
Some Pokémon are common (like Magikarp) and some are rare (like Dratini), just like in the games.

🔁 6. Trade with your brothers!

1
When your Poké Ball is full, you can also choose 🔁 TRADE with a friend (instead of catching).
2
Every time you rank up, you get a 🎟 trade ticket. Tickets are saved in your 🎒 Pokémon box until you use them.
3
Pick the Pokémon you want, then the one you give. Pass the phone: your friend taps ✅ Accept or ❌ Decline.
⭐
A traded Pokémon keeps its level and EXP. If you trade your partner, its EXP goes with it!
👍
If your friend says no, you lose nothing: choose your reward again, or keep your ticket.

  • You get one prize: the highest ball you reach.
  • Look at My progress to see how close you are.
  • ⭐ Trainer rank: every 10% of your goal you go up a rank! Rookie Trainer → Youngster → Bug Catcher → … → Gym Leader (100%) → … → CHAMPION (200%).
  • Tap a Pokémon in your 🎒 Pokémon box, then tap ☆ Partner to choose your partner Pokémon. Your partner gets all the EXP!
  • Taiga & Leo: books AND times tables both count.
  • Prizes are given on Sunday 1 November.

👪 For grown-ups

  • Records are shared on every phone and laptop that opens this app.
  • Typed something wrong? Open 📜 Records and tap ✕ to delete it. Records used in a trade cannot be deleted (the Pokémon would leave the other child's box); fix those in the sheet. Deleting a record also takes away the EXP it gave.
  • Goals can be changed in Settings (for grown-ups) at the bottom.
  • iPhone: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
