HOW TO PLAY

Read books, practise times tables, catch Pokémon and win prizes!

1. Pick your name

👆
At the top, tap TAIGA, LEO or YUDAI. Always check it is your name!

📖 2. Read a book

1
Read a whole book.
2
Tap I read a book!
3
Type the book title.
4
Quiz? Pick No quiz, or take a quiz on Free Book Quiz and type your score (like 8 out of 10). Don't look at the book!
5
Tap THROW POKé BALL! and catch a Pokémon! 🎉 (With a quiz you need 8 out of 10 or more.)

🎸 3. Times Tables (Taiga & Leo)

1
Tap Times Tables, then Open Times Tables Rock Stars.
2
Log in with your school username and password and play.
3
Come back and type how many you got right. Tap LET IT RIP!
✨
The app adds up all your correct answers for you.

🏆 4. Win prizes!

  • You get one prize: the highest ball you reach.
  • Look at My progress to see how close you are.
  • Taiga & Leo: books AND times tables both count.
  • Prizes are given on Sunday 1 November.

👪 For grown-ups

  • Records are shared on every phone and laptop that opens this app.
  • Typed something wrong? Open My collection and tap ✕ to delete it.
  • Goals can be changed in Settings (for grown-ups) at the bottom.
  • iPhone: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
