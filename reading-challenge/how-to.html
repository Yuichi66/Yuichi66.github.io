HOW TO PLAY

Read books, practise times tables, catch Pokémon and win prizes!

1. Pick your name

👆
At the top, tap TAIGA, LEO or YUDAI. Always check it is your name!

📖 2. Read a book

1
Read a whole book.
2
Tap I read a book!
3
Type the book title.
4
Quiz? Pick No quiz, or pick ReadnQuiz, log in, take the quiz and type your score (like 8 out of 10). Don't look at the book!
5
Tap THROW POKé BALL! and pick your reward: catch a NEW Pokémon or evolve one! 🎉 (With a quiz you need 8 out of 10 or more.)

🎸 3. Times Tables (Taiga & Leo)

1
Tap Times Tables, then Open Times Tables Rock Stars.
2
Log in with your school username and password and play.
3
Come back and type how many you got right. Tap LET IT RIP!. Get 50 or more in one go to win a reward! 🎉
✨
The app adds up all your correct answers for you.

🏛️ 4. Latin & 🧮 MyMaths (Yudai)

1
Practise Latin or MyMaths.
2
Tap Latin or MyMaths and type how many you got right.
3
Latin: get 150 or more in one go to win a reward! MyMaths: get 100 or more in one go! 🎉
✨
These catch Pokémon. Your prize comes from books.

✨ 5. Evolve your Pokémon!

1
You win a reward when you:
  • 📖 Read a book (and pass the quiz)
  • 🎸 Times tables: 50 or more right in one go
  • 🏛️ Latin: 150 or more right in one go
  • 🧮 MyMaths: 100 or more right in one go
2
Then YOU choose ONE:
🎯 Catch a NEW Pokémon or ✨ EVOLVE a Pokémon
3
Evolve? Tap the Pokémon you want to grow and watch it change! 1 reward = 1 step.
👀
In your 🎒 Pokémon box, ⬆ shows which Pokémon can still grow. Tap a Pokémon to see how you caught it.
🔥
Keep going! Charmander → Charmeleon → Charizard → Mega Charizard X → Mega Charizard Y → Gigantamax Charizard. Pikachu can become Raichu and even Mega Raichu! You choose which Pokémon to grow.

🔁 6. Trade with your brothers!

1
When you win a reward, you can also choose 🔁 TRADE with a friend (instead of catching or evolving).
2
Every time you rank up, you get a 🎟 trade ticket. Tickets are saved in your 🎒 Pokémon box until you use them.
3
Pick the Pokémon you want, then the one you give. Pass the phone: your friend taps ✅ Accept or ❌ Decline.
👍
If your friend says no, you lose nothing: choose your reward again, or keep your ticket.

🏆 7. Win prizes!

Great Ball
£2
Reach your goal
Ultra Ball
£4
150% of your goal
Master Ball
£6
200% of your goal
  • You get one prize: the highest ball you reach.
  • Look at My progress to see how close you are.
  • ⭐ Trainer rank: every 10% of your goal you go up a rank! Rookie Trainer → Youngster → Bug Catcher → … → Gym Leader (100%) → … → CHAMPION (200%).
  • Tap a Pokémon in your 🎒 Pokémon box, then tap ☆ Partner to choose your partner Pokémon.
  • Taiga & Leo: books AND times tables both count.
  • Prizes are given on Sunday 1 November.

👪 For grown-ups

  • Records are shared on every phone and laptop that opens this app.
  • Typed something wrong? Open 📜 Records and tap ✕ to delete it. Records used in a trade cannot be deleted (the Pokémon would leave the other child's box); fix those in the sheet.
  • Goals can be changed in Settings (for grown-ups) at the bottom.
  • iPhone: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
